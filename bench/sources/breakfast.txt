The Harmon Valley school district will serve free breakfast in every classroom starting in September, after a two-year pilot at four elementary schools.

During the pilot, breakfast participation at those schools rose from 31 percent of students to 78 percent, according to the district's nutrition office.

Teachers at the pilot schools reported 22 percent fewer visits to the nurse's office before lunch, mostly for headaches and stomachaches.

The program will cost about $2.3 million a year, and the district expects federal meal reimbursements to cover all but $180,000 of it.

Breakfast will be delivered to classrooms in insulated bags by 7:50 each morning, and students will eat during the first ten minutes of the school day.

The union representing custodial staff negotiated two additional positions to handle the extra cleanup in classrooms.
